Annabel's Pet Care · Photo wall · Design board, round 1

Five ways to show every real client

Anna has hundreds of real client photos from walks and house sits. Each concept below is a working demo of a different way to put all of them on the site, so a visitor can see this is a real, busy, local business.

Every demo runs with 320 photos so you can feel the speed at real size. All photos are drawn PLACEHOLDER tiles with no pet names. Captions only show service, rough area and month, which is all the real version would show too.

Drag, scroll, pinch and click a photo in each one. Try them on a phone too.

What we're deciding

  1. Which one feels most like Anna? Pick one, or one plus one (for example a homepage strip that opens the full wall).
  2. Its own page (/clients) or a section on the homepage?
  3. Is showing rough areas (SLC, Park City, Snyderville, Heber) OK with her? Never addresses.
  4. Does she need to ask clients before their pet goes on the wall? (Suggested: yes, one text each.)
