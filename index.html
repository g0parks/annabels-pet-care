Anna Q. Founder & Lead Caregiver

Hi, I'm Anna.

I'm 26 years old and have lived with and cared for animals my entire life. My work in professional pet care began at age 15 working at a doggy daycare, and I've been volunteering with local animal shelters ever since.

In high school, I completed the specialized CAPS Veterinary Medicine program, learning animal health, emergency response, and dog/cat CPR. Over the past 12 years—and with hundreds of dogs and cats cared for—I make sure to always go the extra mile to give your pets the love, attention, and quality care they deserve.

Whether your dog needs a midday walk, a long mountain hike, or dedicated care while you're away, I prioritize being available over the phone and sending photo updates so you always know your pets are in good hands.
